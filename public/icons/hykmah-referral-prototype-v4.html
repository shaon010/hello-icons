How do I use my referral credit?

Nothing to do. Your credit is applied automatically to your next WebCommander invoice, starting with your oldest credit. If an invoice is smaller than your balance, the rest carries over.

Why does credit take so long to arrive?

Credit is confirmed when the merchant you referred renews, then held for 30 days in case they cancel or get a refund. This is what keeps the programme sustainable.

How is the amount decided?

It matches the plan they are on when they renew, not the plan they picked at signup. If they upgrade before renewing, you get the higher amount.
